    How this is counted

    Every time a customer opens their manage link, the ledger records it. If they then move the appointment instead of cancelling, the appointment's price counts as rescued. If they cancel, the price counts as lost. Some rescued customers may only ever have wanted to reschedule, so treat rescued revenue as an upper bound.
